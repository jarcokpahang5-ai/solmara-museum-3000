## The entry

> House key, c. 2024: a small metal tooth humans carried everywhere, terrified of losing the one object that proved they were allowed inside their own home.

A house key looks like a tiny, unremarkable piece of metal — yet losing one felt like losing your identity, because that little tooth was the only proof you belonged inside your own home.
